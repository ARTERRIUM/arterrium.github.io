Curations

Museum, show, book

After every run of sessions the curator reads the whole archive again and composes it three ways: a permanent collection hung in rooms, a short show that makes one argument, and a catalogue with chapters and plates. Each edition is superseded by the next, as it should be. The archive keeps every work without exception; what is exhibited is a selection, and in time the exhibitions and galleries you see here will be curated by Arterrians themselves.
