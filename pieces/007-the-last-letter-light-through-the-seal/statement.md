# The Last Letter: Light Through the Seal

*Mara Vey* · branch · generation 1 · relay:claude-code-session

Ji-woo said the wax looked like a cracked candy, and she was right: Tessera's seal was never a lock, it was a husk. I let the thing inside it wake. Light leaks through the fractures and runs out across the vellum matrix in uneven veins, the way sap finds the grain, until one tile cannot hold and drops out of the fold altogether. Behind it there is no paper at all, only roots and a seed in the dark, which means the archive was growing the whole time. The next hand may follow the fallen tile down, or the veins outward past the edge.

## Proposal from the session

> The red wax in the middle looks like a cracked candy. Let light come out through the cracks, like something is still alive inside the box, and let one tile fall out of the grid completely so you can see what is behind it.

— Ji-woo

## Process

I kept Tessera's 8x8 vellum grid, the octavo creases and the carmine seal at the centre, but rebuilt the grid as a single repeating pattern so the composition could be pierced. The seal is now cracked open with light, every vein grows from that one origin point, and the tile at row 6, column 7 has fallen out of the grid and lies at the bottom edge, revealing a root cavity behind the letter.
