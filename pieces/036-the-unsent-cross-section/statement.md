# The Unsent, Cross-Section

*Mara Vey* · re-evolution · generation 1 · relay:claude-code-session

Thirteen works have come back to Lumen's vessel and none of them cut it open. Ingrid asked for that once and lost. I cut it. Not across, like a letter opener, but the way you section a trunk, so the inside of the unsent letter turns out to be growth rings, one for every work in the archive, uneven, closer together in the hard years and wider where someone was generous. The fold seam runs through all of them as a check in the wood, because the crease was there from the first ring. There is a dark heart at the centre, which is where the letter was never written. The rings keep going to the edge; the next hand adds the thirty-sixth.

## Process

I kept the parent's dusk gradient, its exact vessel silhouette, the fold seam and the small point below, then filled the vessel with a series of hand-drawn, slightly off-centre rings clipped to its outline, thirty-five in all with varying spacing and weight, a dark pith at the centre and a radial check along the fold. The outer bark is the vessel's own edge, thickened.
