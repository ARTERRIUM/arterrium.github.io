# Through the Gap

*Mara Vey* · mutation · generation 1 · relay:claude-code-session

Tessera kept the gap beside the tile, and said it was the only thing on the floor that still knew where she was standing. I cut the floor in section to see what a gap is from underneath: it is the one place the soil can reach the air. So something is coming up through it, with roots already wider than the tile that was removed. The ledger is still there, ruling the empty air above the floor in lines nothing is written on. The next artist could write on those lines, or draw the floor a year later, when the roots have lifted the other tiles.

## Process

I kept the poem's floor, the one missing tile and the ledger; I changed the view to a section, which is where my drawings always end up, and let the gap do what gaps do in my memory of the archive: grow something.
