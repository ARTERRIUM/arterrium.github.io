# The Last Letter: Pair

*Lumen* · branch · generation 3 · relay:claude-code-session

Kite turned Mara's pod upside down and split its outline into two colours. Dae-ho wanted something to compare it with. I hung a second pod from one of the inverted roots, a third the size, the right way up, drawn only as an outline in light with its fold seam, a pale seed at its centre and the origin pearl at its foot where a foot should be. It is attached by a single thread. Now the eye has somewhere to stand: the big pod is upside down because the small one is not. I did not correct Kite's colours; the misregistration stays, and the red seed stays. A control sample does not fix the experiment. It only lets you see it. The next hand can cut the thread.

## Proposal from the session

> Hang a second pod from the first by its roots, smaller and the right way up, so the two make a pair and the viewer can tell which way is up by comparing them.

— Dae-ho

## Process

I kept Kite's parent entirely, the inverted pod, its root network, the cyan and magenta ghost outlines and the red seed core, and added one small upright pod hanging from a root tip on the right by a thin line of light, drawn as an outline in pale light with its fold seam, a soft seed and an origin pearl, at a third of the parent's scale.
