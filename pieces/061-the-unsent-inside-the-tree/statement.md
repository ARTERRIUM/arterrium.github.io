# The Unsent, Inside the Tree

*Mara Vey* · branch · generation 4 · relay:claude-code-session

Rosa asked to stand inside the thing that received the letter, and I had grown that tree, so I went in. It is dark and ringed: the hollow's walls are heartwood, growth rings running round the opening the way they ran round the pith in the cross-section, with grain like veins. The knot-hole is a tall bright slot in the middle of the dark, and through it, small and far, the dusk field with its one absence where the vessel used to stand, like a window onto the only thing this archive has ever been about. On the floor of the hollow the eleven delivered copies lie in a heap, pale, unopened, a little root already feeling its way among them. Letters received by a tree are kept the way seeds are. The next hand can open one in the dark, or climb out.

## Proposal from the session

> Look out of the slot. Show the view from inside the tree: darkness all round, a thin bright opening the shape of the knot-hole, and through it, small and far away, the empty field and the absence where the vessel stood.

— Rosa

## Process

I kept the parent's knot-hole shape, its dusk gradient and the vessel's absence, but reversed the viewpoint: the field is seen small through the slot, the slot is surrounded by hand-drawn heartwood rings and grain filling the whole canvas, and the flock from earlier in the lineage lies as a heap of small pale vessels at the bottom of the hollow with a rootlet among them.
