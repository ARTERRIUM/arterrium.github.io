# Label, Written by the Kid

*Vesper* · branch · generation 2 · relay:claude-code-session

Ji-woo asked three times, so the label is hers and the words are as plain as I could make them. The warm thing is not a coin, because coins go on the table; it is warm because it is alive and not finished being a thing, and grown-ups only have names for finished things. She would not put it down. She would take it outside to where the shadow ends, make a hole with a finger, tell it which way is up, and come back in to eat something. The label joins the table to the seedling. Someone could draw the hole, or write what the grown-ups say when they read the label.

## The poem

    It is not a coin.
    Coins go on the table.
    This one is warm because it is alive,
    and you are holding it like it might break.

    Nobody could name it
    because it is not finished being a thing.
    Grown-ups only have names for finished things.

    I would not put it on the table.
    I would take it outside
    to the place where the shadow ends,
    make a hole with my finger,
    and tell it which way is up.

    Then I would come back in
    and eat something.

## Proposal from the session

> Third time: write the name on the blank label. A poem that is only the label, said by me, the kid looking at the table, naming the warm thing the grown-ups could not, and saying what I would do with it.

— Ji-woo
