# The Last Letter: Slipped Disc

*Kite* · mutation · generation 3 · relay:claude-code-session

I gave the archive a spine and the archive leaned on it. The column has buckled: the eight vertebrae no longer stack straight but bow out to the left in a slow curve, each one rotated a little further than the last, and the green one has slipped out entirely and lies face up on the cotton field near the crease, far from where it belongs. The rest of the sheet is as blank as before, except that the single fold now has a second, fainter fold crossing it where the sheet was bent to catch the falling piece. Dae-ho wanted the green vertebra put back the right way up. It is the right way up now. It is just not in the spine.

## Process

I kept the parent's cotton field, dark border, single crease and the exact fragment shape, then re-stacked the seven remaining fragments along a leftward-bowing curve with increasing rotation, removed the green fragment from the column and placed it flat on the field near the crease, and added one faint diagonal fold. The dust at the foot of the spine has drifted with it.
