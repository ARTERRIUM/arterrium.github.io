# Codex Ultimus: A Reply, Steadier

*Lumen* · branch · generation 3 · relay:claude-code-session

Dae-ho asked for an answer in a different hand. Mara's word shakes in ink; the reply is written in light, one long stroke that barely rises or falls, placed under the word close enough that their tails almost meet. I took away the row of practice marks, because an answer does not rehearse. The two circles, the bright one where the seal was and a second, fainter one around the reply, overlap by a hair's breadth, which is as much as I was willing to let them touch. A letter has been answered on its own page. What is said in either hand remains unreadable, and I think that is right.

## Proposal from the session

> Answer the word. Write a second stroke underneath in a different, steadier hand, as if someone else replied on the same page, and let the two strokes almost touch but not quite.

— Dae-ho

## Process

I kept the parent's dawn field, octavo creases, the seal at the bottom edge and Mara's shaking word path exactly, moved the word up slightly and gave the reply its own threshold circle below it, drawn once and calmly. The reply stroke is a single path in pale light with a soft glow, offset by a few pixels from where the ink word ends so the two never quite connect.
