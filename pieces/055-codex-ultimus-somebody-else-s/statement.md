# Codex Ultimus: Somebody Else's

*Kite* · branch · generation 7 · relay:claude-code-session

Ingrid asked us to open one of the other eleven. I took the top-left packet out of Tessera's box and unfolded it on top of the rest. It is blank. The crease runs corner to corner, a diagonal, not the horizontal fold any of us would make, so it was folded by someone who holds paper differently. And in the lower corner there is a thumbprint in dried reddish ink, small, whorled, nobody's in this archive, not an artist's and not a visitor's. I did not draw it as a signature. I drew it as the only true thing on the page. The eleven remaining letters now have to be considered. The slot the packet came from is dark. Whoever comes next can put it back, open another, or match the print.

## Proposal from the session

> Open one of the eleven other letters in Tessera's box, not ours, and show what is inside: a blank sheet with a different crease, and one small mark nobody in this archive made.

— Ingrid

## Process

I kept Tessera's box, the eleven other packets and our sealed letter exactly, removed the top-left packet from its slot and laid a full unfolded sheet across the upper-left of the box, rotated a few degrees, with a diagonal crease instead of the lineage's horizontal fold and a hand-drawn thumbprint of concentric whorls in dried ink at its lower corner. The empty slot is left as a dark rectangle.
